Explore Rajasthan

Jaipur — The Pink City

Jaipur is famous for the beautiful Hawa Mahal, royal palaces, forts and colourful markets. It is a perfect destination to experience Rajasthan's rich history and culture.

Udaipur — The City of Lakes

Udaipur is famous for its beautiful lakes, peaceful sunset views, City Palace and royal architecture. Lake Pichola is one of its most popular attractions.

Jaisalmer — The Golden City

Jaisalmer is famous for the Thar Desert, golden sand dunes, camel safaris and the magnificent Jaisalmer Fort. It offers a unique desert travel experience.
